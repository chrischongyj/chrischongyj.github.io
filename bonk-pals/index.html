Bonk Pals

A cute party brawler for phones

Grab your frisbee and bonk your pals! Bonk Pals is an online arena game for up to four players, with bots filling any empty spots. One hit and you're out; first to five rounds wins.

How it plays

  • One frisbee each. Swat up close, or throw it and bank it off walls. Hold to charge a faster throw, then call it back, bonking anyone in its path.
  • Clash! Swat an incoming frisbee out of the air, or meet a swing with a swing.
  • Power-ups such as frost, fire and explosive discs, a psychic disc you steer, missile strikes, banana peels, ghost mode and bubble shields.
  • Eight arenas with traps: cliffs to fall off, a hedge maze that reshapes, a runaway mower, a pool with sprinklers, a bakery with conveyor belts and presses, and a mini golf course with a windmill.

Twelve pals, lots of outfits

Quokka, Crab, Raccoon, Penguin, Porcupine, Frog, Capybara, Red Panda, Axolotl, Otter, Duck and Sloth all play the same, so pick your favourite and dress it up. Earn coins every match and spend them on hats, glasses, capes, animated effects and new frisbees.

Playing

Practice against bots without an account. Sign in with Apple, Google or email to play online, create private rooms for friends, earn rank points and climb the leaderboards.
