Bonk Pals

A cute arena brawler for phones

Pick a pal, grab power-ups and bonk the others until one is left standing. Bonk Pals is a fast online arena game for up to four players, with bots filling any empty spots.

The pals

  • Quokka throws a boomerang that hits on the way out and on the way back.
  • Crab pinches up close and charges through anyone in the way.
  • Raccoon snipes with a slingshot and vanishes in a puff of smoke.
  • Penguin lobs snowballs over walls and belly-slides into fights.
  • Porcupine sprays quills and curls into a spiky ball.
  • Frog grabs enemies with its tongue and squashes them with a mega hop.

Playing

Practice against bots without an account. Sign in with Apple or Google to play online, choose your player name and earn rank points.
