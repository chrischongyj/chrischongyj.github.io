How to play

  • Left thumb moves. Right thumb: drag to aim and release to attack, or tap to auto-aim at the nearest enemy.
  • Hold the attack to charge a stronger shot (quokka, raccoon, penguin and frog). You move slower while charging.
  • The yellow button is your pal's skill. It recharges over time.
  • Walk over power-ups or smash crates to find them.
  • The last pal standing wins the round. First to three rounds wins the match.
  • Stay inside the circle: the arena shrinks as the round goes on.
