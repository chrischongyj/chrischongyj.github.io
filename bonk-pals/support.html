Bonk Pals

Bonk Pals is a party brawler for up to four players. Every pal has the same frisbee: swat it, throw it, bounce it off walls and call it back. One hit and you're out. First to win five rounds takes the match.

Need help or found a bug?
Email chrisproductions7@gmail.com and tell me what happened. Mentioning your phone model and iOS or Android version helps me track it down.

How to play

  • Left thumb: run around.
  • Tap the right side: swat with your frisbee, up close. Swatting an incoming frisbee knocks it out of the air.
  • Drag the right side and let go: throw. Hold longer for a faster, longer throw. Frisbees bounce off walls.
  • No frisbee? Tap to call it back. It knocks out anyone in the way, but walls stop it, so move where it can see you.
  • Dash button: a quick burst for dodging.
  • Power-ups appear around the arena. You can hold one at a time; picking up another replaces it.
  • Stay inside the dotted line. Three seconds outside it and you're out.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No for practice against computer players. Yes for online matches, private rooms with friends, rank points and coins.

How do I play with friends?
Tap Play, then Create room, and share the 5-letter code. Friends tap Play and enter the code to join. Empty seats are filled by computer players.

How do I get coins?
Every match pays coins, more for better finishes and for knockouts. Spend them in the shop on weapons and effects. They're looks only; every pal plays the same.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what is removed.

Someone left my match.
Their pal is knocked out for the rest of the match. If everyone else leaves, you win.
