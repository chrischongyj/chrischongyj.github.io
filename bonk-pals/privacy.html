Privacy Policy

Bonk Pals · Last updated 4 November 2026

Bonk Pals is an online party brawler. This page explains exactly what the game stores, why, and how to delete it. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app, and nothing is ever sold or shared for marketing.

Playing without an account

You can play practice matches against computer players without signing in. Nothing about you is stored for those matches. Your device connects to a game server so the match can run, which means the server briefly sees your device's IP address, the same as any website you visit.

If you sign in

Online matches, rank points, coins and outfits need an account. You can sign in with Google, with Apple, or with an email address. When you do, we store:

  • Your email address and an account ID, provided by Google, Apple or the email sign-in, plus your name if Google or Apple shares it. Used only to sign you in. Never shown to other players, never emailed for marketing. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the private relay address.
  • The display name you choose. Other players see it in matches and on the leaderboards.
  • Your game progress: rank points, matches played, wins, knockouts, which pals you play, coins, items you bought with coins, and the outfits you equip. Your name and points appear on public leaderboards.
  • Match records: the finishing places and scores of each online match, so points and coins are awarded correctly.

Coins are earned by playing. The app has no real-money purchases.

During online matches

To play online your device connects to our game servers. They see your IP address and the inputs you send (movement, aiming and attacks) in order to run the match. Server logs used to fix problems are kept for up to 14 days.

Where it's stored

Accounts and game data are stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Singapore. Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito; game data is kept in Amazon DynamoDB; game servers run on Amazon GameLift. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, that company's own privacy policy applies to the sign-in step.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your sign-in, email address, name, coins, items, outfits, stats and leaderboard places. Past match records keep only a meaningless account ID that no longer links to you.

Can't get into the app? Email the address below from the address you signed in with, and the account will be deleted for you within 30 days.

Children

Bonk Pals is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
