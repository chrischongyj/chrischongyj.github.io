Privacy Policy

Bonk Pals · Last updated 4 November 2026

Bonk Pals collects only what it needs to run online matches and your player account. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold.

Playing without an account

You can play practice matches without signing in. A temporary, anonymous session is created on our game servers for the match and no personal information is collected.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Apple or Google. When you do, we receive:

  • A unique account identifier from Apple or Google
  • Your email address (with Apple, this may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email)
  • Your name, if Apple or Google shares it

We use these only to keep you signed in and to recognise your account. We do not email you marketing messages.

What the game stores

  • The player name you choose, which other players can see
  • Your match results and rank points, used for leaderboards
  • Your critter customisations

During online matches

To play online your device connects to our game servers. They see your IP address and the inputs you send (movement, aiming and attacks) in order to run the match. Server logs used to fix problems are kept for up to 14 days.

Where data is kept

Accounts and game data are stored with Amazon Web Services (Amazon Cognito, DynamoDB and GameLift) in the Asia Pacific (Singapore) region. Sign-in is provided by Apple and Google, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your account

To delete your account and all of its data, email chrisproductions7@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or use the delete-account option in the app once it is available. Your account is deleted within 30 days.

Children

Bonk Pals does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children can play practice matches without an account. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
