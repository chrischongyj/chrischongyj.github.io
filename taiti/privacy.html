Privacy Policy

Taiti: Big Two · Last updated 1 November 2026

Taiti: Big Two does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers of any kind.

What the app stores

The app saves a few settings so the game remembers how you like to play:

  • The name you type in
  • Your chosen game mode and difficulty
  • Whether sound is on or off

All of this is stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage. It is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

Internet access

The app does not connect to the internet. It contains no networking code and will work perfectly with your device in airplane mode.

Third parties

No third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services are built into this app. Nothing about your use of it is shared with anyone, because nothing about your use of it leaves your device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
