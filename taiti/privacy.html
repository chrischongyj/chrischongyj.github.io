Privacy Policy

AH Taiti · Last updated 7 November 2026

AH Taiti is a Big Two card game you can play against the computer or online. This page explains exactly what the game stores, why, and how to delete it. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app, and nothing is ever sold or shared for marketing.

Playing the computer

Games against computer players work offline and need no account. The app saves a few settings on your device only (the name you type, your game mode and difficulty, and whether sound is on). They are never sent anywhere, and deleting the app removes them.

If you sign in

Online games, rankings, coins, card designs and friends need an account. You can sign in with Google, with Apple, or with an email address. The same account works in our other game, Bonk Pals. When you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address and an account ID, provided by Google, Apple or the email sign-in, plus your name if Google or Apple shares it. Used only to sign you in. Never shown to other players, never emailed for marketing. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the private relay address.
  • The display name you choose. Other players see it at the table and on the leaderboards.
  • Your game progress: rank points, matches played and won, deals won, which game mode you play, coins, the card designs you bought with coins and the ones you use. Your name and points appear on public leaderboards.
  • Match records: the finishing places and scores of each online match, so points and coins are awarded correctly.
  • Which days you open the app (the date only), so we can see how many players come back and improve the game. It is never used for advertising or shared.
  • Your friends: your friend code, the friends you add, friend requests and game invites. Your friends can see your display name, whether you're online or in a game, and when you were last online. Nobody else can.
  • Daily rewards and seasons: your check-in streak, daily quest progress, and your points and tier in each monthly season.

Coins are earned by playing. The app has no real-money purchases and no gambling: coins can't be bought, sold or cashed out, and only buy card designs.

During online games

To play online your device connects to our game servers. They see your IP address and the cards you play in order to run the game. Each player is only ever sent their own cards. There is no chat; players can only send a few preset emotes. Server logs used to fix problems are kept for up to 14 days.

Where it's stored

Accounts and game data are stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Singapore. Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito; game data is kept in Amazon DynamoDB; game servers run on Amazon GameLift. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, that company's own privacy policy applies to the sign-in step.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your sign-in, email address, name, coins, card designs, stats, leaderboard places, friend code and friends list (you also disappear from your friends' lists), in AH Taiti and Bonk Pals alike. Past match records keep only a meaningless account ID that no longer links to you.

Can't get into the app? Email the address below from the address you signed in with, and the account will be deleted for you within 30 days.

Children

Online play is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.
