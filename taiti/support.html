AH Taiti

AH Taiti is a Big Two card game. Play three computer players offline, or sign in with Google or Apple to play Big Two and AH Taiti online: quick ranked matches, private rooms with friends, daily rewards, monthly seasons and card designs to collect.

How to play

  • Cards rank 3 (low) up through K, A, and 2 (high). Suits rank ♦ < ♣ < ♥ < ♠.
  • Whoever holds the 3♦ leads first and must play it.
  • Play singles, pairs or five-card hands. Triples on their own are not allowed.
  • Beat what is on the table using the same number of cards, or pass. Once you pass, you are out until the next lead.
  • Five-card hands rank: straight < flush < full house < four of a kind (plus a side card) < straight flush.
  • Straights do not wrap around. J-Q-K-A-2 is the highest straight.

AH Taiti mode

  • Play carries on until only one player is left holding cards. Finishing places are President, Vice-President, Vice-Scum and Scum, worth 3, 2, 1 and 0 points.
  • Before the next deal, cards are swapped automatically. The President hands their 2 worst cards to the Scum and receives their 2 best. The Vice-President and Vice-Scum swap one card the same way.
  • The flip: when the Scum wins a trick, they may flip the order. 3 becomes the highest card, ♦ becomes the top suit, and the five-card hand rankings reverse. It lasts for the rest of the deal, and the Scum can flip it back on a later trick.

Common questions

Can I play against friends?
Yes. Sign in, then tap Play online → Create room and share the room code, or add each other in Friends and tap Play. Empty seats are filled by computer players, so you can play with just one friend.

How does an online match work?
A match is five deals. In Big Two the fewest penalty points wins; in AH Taiti the most points wins. You have 20 seconds a turn; if time runs out the computer plays for you until you tap I'm back. If you leave, a computer player finishes your seat and you place last.

What are coins for?
Coins come from playing, daily check-ins and quests. They buy card backs and decks in the shop. They can't be bought with real money or cashed out.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile → Delete account. See the privacy policy for what is removed.

What do the difficulty levels do?
Easy plays its lowest cards and sometimes passes for no reason. Normal plans its hand, saves its 2s, and blocks players about to go out. Hard remembers every card played and thinks ahead in the endgame, using only the information any player at the table can see.

Does the game use my data?
Playing the computer collects nothing. If you sign in to play online, the game stores your account, display name, progress and friends to run online play. There are no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
